Introduction
What FactoryOS is
A single platform that replaces the spreadsheets, point tools, and tribal knowledge a plant's leadership, production, maintenance, safety, and quality teams would otherwise maintain separately.

FactoryOS is a real-time factory operations command center built for discrete and process manufacturing plants. It consolidates the metrics a plant would normally track across disconnected tools — production throughput, machine condition, operator performance, order fulfillment, safety incidents, environmental compliance, and energy consumption — into thirteen purpose-built live dashboards, plus a navigable 3D digital twin of the physical factory floor.

Every screen refreshes on its own live cycle, so a number on the page is never more than seconds stale. The platform is architected data-source-agnostic: today's build runs on a realistic simulation layer, with every data type already shaped so a production ERP, MES, SCADA system, or IoT/computer-vision pipeline can be connected later without redesigning a single screen.

Brand identity — name, tagline, logo, and accent color — is runtime-configurable from within the product itself, making FactoryOS deployable as a genuine multi-tenant SaaS offering: the same codebase serves any manufacturing customer under its own identity.

Who it serves

Plant leadership — one roll-up screen for revenue, efficiency, quality, safety, and energy.

Production & operations managers — line-level throughput, OEE, and downtime.

Maintenance & reliability engineers — condition-based asset health and work-order compliance.

EHS teams — incident, near-miss, and environmental-limit tracking.

Security & floor operations — live spatial awareness of every tracked worker and asset.

Built on

React 18 + TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Recharts for 2D visualization, and three.js / React Three Fiber for the live 3D factory model — a modern, maintainable stack chosen for both performance and long-term extensibility.

Platform Workflows
From signal to action
Two representative end-to-end workflows the platform supports today, as implemented — not aspirational.
Workflow 1 — Restricted-Zone Response (Spatial Intelligence)
1
Alert fires
A worker enters the Chemical Store restricted zone; the 3D Factory Monitoring Active Alerts feed logs it in real time.
2
Operator clicks the name
The flagged worker's name is clicked in the live Worker Presence list.
3
Camera flies to location
The 3D camera animates to the worker's exact position in the model for visual confirmation.
4
Confirm via CCTV
The nearest camera marker's online status is checked to confirm footage is available.
5
Respond
Floor operations dispatches a response with full spatial context already established.
Workflow 2 — New Customer Onboarding (Administration)
1
Open Settings
An administrator onboarding a new customer navigates to the Settings page.
2

Brand name, tagline, and full/legal name are entered.

Upload logo
The customer's logo replaces the default mark.

Accent color and light/dark mode are set to match the customer's brand.

All 13 dashboards re-render under the new identity instantly — no redeploy.
